The line drawing at the top of the home page is a small patch of water: eleven lines seen from a low angle, lifted by ripples travelling out from one point. Click anywhere on it to drop something in: a single shockwave ring races out and fades, like the dithered logo on [linear.app/next](https://linear.app/next), and the ripples move there with a kick that settles back into the usual motion while the old ones drift away. Hovering presses a soft dent into the surface.
