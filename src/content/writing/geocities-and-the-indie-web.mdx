---
title: "When we had a place on the internet"
date: 2026-09-23
description: "GeoCities, Neocities, and the people bringing back the handcrafted web."
---

If you were around in the early days of the internet, the first picture that comes to mind when you think of personal websites back then is probably a page in GeoCities.

They weren't exactly great in terms of UX or UI. Pages were crowded, full of GIFS and using color palettes that would make your eyes bleed.

But you could tell a real person was behind them. People would handcraft these sites with passion to share their hobbies, interests and thoughts with the world.

The internet today is very different. Most of us just have a profile on someone else's platform, and everything is fed to us through a feed.

Back then, you had to go looking for things. You had to find them yourself, usually through a friend, a forum or even a magazine.

Recently, I found out that some people are trying to bring that spirit back, and one of the main places they're doing it is [Neocities](https://neocities.org/).

Browsing through Neocities feels like the old web, when the internet was about leaving your mark and showing the world you existed. People link to their friends with tiny buttons, and build pages that are a little messy and very much theirs.

It's nice to have a place again.
