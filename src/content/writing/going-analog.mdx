---
title: "Going Analog"
date: 2026-02-10
description: "When everything competes for attention."
---

As someone who grew up in the 90s, I still find something special about the tech from that era.

Walking to the video store on Fridays to pick one VHS for the weekend. Bringing my Game Boy to school to trade Pokémon. Making mixtapes for my Walkman. Wearing a Casio F91-W and thinking the green backlight was the coolest thing ever.

Maybe it's nostalgia. But back then, you picked something and stayed with it. I think those experiences felt special because there were fewer distractions, fewer conveniences, and fewer alternatives competing for attention.

Lately, I've been going back to that. I even picked up a CRT (damn, those things are heavy) just to hook up my old consoles.

Not because it's better. It's a deliberate choice to slow down and embrace a little inefficiency.

There's something clarifying about a device that does one thing and does it without asking for anything back.
